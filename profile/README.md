# JRF Desk

**UGC NET, PhD, JRF & SET — explained from the official rules.**

[jrfdesk.com](https://jrfdesk.com) helps Indian postgraduates prepare for UGC NET and plan what comes after it:

- **Exam and results:** dates, eligibility, cut-offs for every subject, and how NTA calculates results
- **Free practice:** real past NET questions (Paper 1 and Economics) with NTA's final answer key and worked explanations
- **After you qualify:** PhD admission, the JRF fellowship, state SETs and lecturer recruitment
- **Careers for economists:** RBI Grade B (DEPR), SEBI Grade A (Research) and the Indian Economic Service

Every fact on the site links to the official NTA, UGC, UPSC, RBI, SEBI or university document it comes from.
Not affiliated with NTA, UGC or any university.
